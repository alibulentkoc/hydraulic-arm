# Changelog

## 2026-10-28

- The rover twin is now the Rover Hydraulic Arm twin (pick-place-twin-rover.html). It is not tied to one rover brand: the arm and the harvest module fit any rover frame.
- Course name: AGM 4060 Mechanical and Hydraulic Systems.
- Lab and tractor twin: notation matches the course documents (y up, triangle ratios); Show answers switch; length-change readout; force sign check with dual counterbalance valves; collision fixes.

## 2026-10-25

- Lab and tractor twin: numbered panel with Required and Optional steps; Counterbalance valves folder (setting as a ratio or in psi, pilot ratio, leakage); Bin and pick width folder with a drawn bin, drop point and rim clearance check of the front of the fruit; Show worst point now searches the full work path (pick, lift, carry and drop); readouts for the pressure in each cylinder, the forces and pressures at the worst point, and a counterbalance guide.
- Rover Hydraulic Arm twin: reorganized panel; the wrist turns back to 0 degrees when the gripper holds the fruit; the stowed conveyor moves 8 in farther with the wrist fitted.
- Help pages rewritten for the current panels, in Simplified Technical English, with equations that contain no unit constants.

## 2026-10-22

- Rover Hydraulic Arm twin: leaf canopy with coverage from 0 to 100%, continuous air knife and wrist puff, finding watermelons while driving, round and elongated fruit, wrist rotation with a worm gearmotor, upgraded proportional hydraulics with a live parallel circuit and parts list, removable harvest module on stands, collision-free layout, reach region toggle, plain-language panel.
- Lab and tractor twin: unchanged.
- Added help pages and the GitHub Pages landing page.
